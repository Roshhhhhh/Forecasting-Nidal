Redesign the existing “New Forecast Request” form to make it much faster, simpler, and easier for internal users to submit a forecast request.

The current form asks for too much detail at the request stage.

The purpose of this form is NOT to create the full forecast.

It is only to collect the minimum information required for the Revenue Manager to understand:

1. Who is the owner
2. What is the property
3. Where is the property
4. What management commission is being proposed
5. Who referred the owner, if applicable
6. Any useful supporting media or notes

The Revenue Manager will later review the request, assign themselves or another representative, complete the full property profile, pricing assumptions, ADRs, LTR benchmark, expenses, amenities, and generate the forecast.

5. Area is a searchable dropdown.
