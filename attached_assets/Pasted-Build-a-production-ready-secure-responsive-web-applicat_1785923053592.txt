Build a production-ready, secure, responsive web application for Royal Holiday Homes called:

RHH Property Revenue Forecaster
Optional product name: Royal Forecast

The platform must allow Royal Holiday Homes employees to create professional short-term rental revenue forecasts, compare them with long-term rental income, generate AI-assisted pricing recommendations, manually override all suggestions, and publish outstanding interactive proposals through secure shareable owner links.

This is not only a calculator. It is an internal forecasting, proposal-generation, sales-conversion, approval, and owner-presentation platform.

==================================================
1. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
==================================================

The platform should help Royal Holiday Homes:

1. Assess a property’s short-term rental potential.
2. Estimate achievable ADR by season.
3. Estimate gross annual short-term rental revenue.
4. estimate operating expenses and management fees.
5. Calculate expected annual and monthly net owner income.
6. Compare short-term rental performance with traditional long-term leasing.
7. Produce multiple occupancy and performance scenarios.
8. Generate a visually premium proposal personalized to each owner.
9. Allow the proposal to be shared using a secure dynamic web link.
10. Track whether the owner opened, viewed, downloaded, accepted, rejected, or requested a call regarding the proposal.
11. Convert an accepted proposal into an onboarding opportunity or CRM lead.
12. Maintain a reliable internal database of Abu Dhabi areas, projects, ADR benchmarks, LTR benchmarks, seasonal pricing and property characteristics.

10. FORECAST CALCULATION ENGINE
==================================================

Create a transparent financial calculation engine.

Do not rely only on one simple annual average ADR calculation.

The preferred calculation should be performed monthly and, when possible, daily.

A. Monthly gross short-term revenue

For each month:

Available Nights =
Calendar Nights
minus Owner Blocked Nights
minus Maintenance Blocked Nights
minus Other Unavailable Nights

Occupied Nights =
Available Nights × Monthly Occupancy Rate

Monthly Gross Revenue =
Occupied Nights × Applicable Monthly Average ADR

Annual Gross Revenue =
Sum of Monthly Gross Revenue from January to December

B. Weighted average annual ADR

Weighted Average ADR =
Total Forecast Room Revenue ÷ Total Forecast Occupied Nights

Do not calculate annual ADR using an unweighted arithmetic average of seasonal ADR values.

C. Seasonal ADR method

Where daily calculation is enabled:

Daily Revenue =
Applicable Daily ADR × Occupancy Probability

Main-event dates override peak, shoulder and low season dates.

Where only seasonal inputs are provided:

Annual Gross Revenue =
Low-Season Available Nights × Low-Season Occupancy × Low-Season ADR
+
Shoulder-Season Available Nights × Shoulder-Season Occupancy × Shoulder ADR
+
Peak-Season Available Nights × Peak-Season Occupancy × Peak ADR
+
Main-Event Available Nights × Main-Event Occupancy × Main-Event ADR

D. Long-term rental benchmark

Gross LTR Income =
Annual Long-Term Rental

Adjusted LTR Income =
Annual Long-Term Rental × (1 - LTR Vacancy Percentage)

Default LTR vacancy percentage can be 10%, but it must be editable.

Allow optional LTR costs such as:
- Leasing commission
- Vacancy loss
- Maintenance
- Property management
- Renewal fees
- Other owner costs

Net LTR Income =
Adjusted LTR Income minus applicable LTR costs

E. Short-term rental expenses

Support the following expense types:

Fixed annual expenses:
- Internet and phone
- Base utility cost
- Maintenance allowance
- Insurance
- Software or smart lock subscriptions
- Miscellaneous expenses
- Other annual costs

Variable expenses:
- Utilities per occupied night
- Utilities percentage of gross revenue
- Maintenance percentage of gross revenue
- Consumables per stay
- Laundry per stay
- Cleaning per stay
- Payment processing percentage
- OTA commission
- Tourism or municipality charges
- Other variable costs

Management fees:
- Percentage of gross revenue
- Percentage of net revenue
- Fixed monthly amount
- Hybrid structure

Default RHH model:
Management Fee = Gross Annual Revenue × RHH PM Commission Percentage

All expense inclusions must be configurable because some costs may be charged directly to guests, absorbed by RHH, or paid by the owner.

F. Total expenses

Total Annual Expenses =
Internet
+ Utilities
+ Maintenance
+ Miscellaneous
+ Other Owner Expenses
+ RHH Management Fee
+ Any Additional Enabled Expense

G. Net owner income

Net Annual Owner Income =
Gross Annual Revenue - Total Annual Expenses

Average Monthly Net Payout =
Net Annual Owner Income ÷ 12

H. Increase versus long-term rental

Increase Amount =
Net Annual STR Income - Net Annual LTR Income

Increase Percentage =
(Net Annual STR Income ÷ Net Annual LTR Income) - 1

Handle a zero or missing LTR value safely and do not show invalid percentages.

I. Scenario engine

Create configurable scenarios such as:

- Conservative
- Realistic
- Confident
- Optimistic

Default occupancy examples:
- 75%
- 80%
- 85%
- 90%

The admin can also enable:
- 60%
- 65%
- 70%
- 95%
- 100%
- Custom occupancy

Each scenario may have its own:

- Occupancy
- ADR multiplier

- Owner usage
- Maintenance reserve
- Management fee
- Selected status
- Internal recommendation

The system should identify one scenario as:

11. DATA VALIDATION AND FINANCIAL QUALITY CONTROLS
==================================================

Add strong validation rules.

Examples:

- Size must fall within a reasonable configurable range.
- Bedroom and bathroom values cannot be negative.
- ADR cannot be negative.
- Occupancy must be between 0% and 100%.
- Commission percentage must be within an approved range.
- Annual utilities that appear unusually high must trigger a warning.
- Annual internet expenses that appear unusually high must trigger a warning.
- LTR must not be treated as zero when it is unknown.
- “NA” must remain unavailable, not zero.
- Gross revenue must equal the total of monthly revenue.
- Net revenue must reconcile to gross revenue minus expenses.
- Scenario comparison values must use the same expense methodology.
- The proposal should not be publishable when required fields are missing.
- A forecast cannot be approved when calculations fail reconciliation checks.
- Show the calculation formula and source behind every important number.

Add outlier detection.

For example:
- If a suggested ADR is more than a configurable percentage above the project benchmark, require a written justification.
- If annual utilities exceed a configurable percentage of annual gross revenue, show an alert.
- If size does not reasonably match the selected bedroom category, show a warning.
- If LTR or ADR varies significantly from the market benchmark, show a warning.

These controls are important because previous proposal examples contain potential demo-data or formatting anomalies. The new platform must prevent unrealistic cost, area or duplicated presentation values from being published without review.
